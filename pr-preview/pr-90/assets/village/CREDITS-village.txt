Village art for Nightwatch (public/assets/village/), built by scripts/village/build_village.py
from the LPC packs below. Changes: tiles and parts cut out and packed, colours graded darker,
the brick walls of the example houses recoloured to timber and stone, doors, windows and signs
placed on them, windows filled with warm light, a lantern hung on a pole, a camp fire set into the
rim of a well.
These files are shared under CC-BY-SA 3.0 (every pack used allows that).

Sources (OpenGameArt):
  [LPC] Terrains                      https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-terrains
  [LPC] Roofs (v2, with its example houses and [LPC] Colonial Buildings)
                                      https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-roofs
  [LPC] Windows & Doors (v2)          https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-windows-doors
  [LPC] Medieval Village Decorations  https://opengameart.org/node/90157
  [LPC] Blacksmith                    https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-blacksmith

Credits of each pack follow, as the packs require.
